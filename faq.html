Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add curved text?

See Curved Text for detailed instructions.

Does this app support layers? How do I create them?

Yes, Paint S supports layers, which are referred to as Objects. Whenever you draw a shape, add text, or paste an image, a new object (layer) is created automatically. You do not need to create layers manually.

How do I open a specific page of a PDF file?

Paint S opens the first page of a PDF and converts it to an image. To edit a different page, open the PDF in macOS Preview, select the desired page, and choose Edit > Copy. Then, switch to Paint S and choose File > New from Clipboard.

How do I add and use a custom gradient?

See Choose a Gradient.

What is the difference between the Free and Pro versions?

The Free version includes basic editing tools but has limitations, such as a maximum image size, watermarks on saved images, and restricted access to advanced features (like curved text, shadows, reflections, gradients, and filters). Upgrading to the Pro version unlocks all features and removes watermarks.

Why wasn't a new app installed after I purchased the Pro version?

The upgrade is an in-app purchase. Once purchased, your existing app automatically unlocks all Pro features. A separate app is not downloaded.

Why are some menu commands greyed out?

Many commands (like filters or transformations) require an active selection. Select an object or an area of the canvas first, and the relevant commands will become available.

How do I restore my purchase?

If your Pro features are missing, delete the app and reinstall it from the App Store. Your purchase should restore automatically.

If the issue persists, choose Help > Feedback issues... to generate a report and email it to support@trybestapps.com.

Tips

Ensure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I change the app language?

You can change the language for specific apps in your macOS System Settings. See Apple Support.

Tips

If the app is open, you may need to quit and reopen it to see the change.

How do I change the number of recent files shown?

Adjust this in your macOS System Settings. See Apple Support.

How do I keep the Color Picker open?

See Pin the Color Picker.

How do I apply effects to a specific part of an image?

  1. Use a Selection Tool to highlight the desired area.
  2. Choose Object > Apply Effect from the menu bar, or right-click the selection and choose Effect.

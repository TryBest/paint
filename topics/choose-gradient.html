Choose a Gradient

You can apply gradients to various elements in Paint S, including:

  • Lines and borders

  • Fills and backgrounds

  • Text

The Gradient Picker

Click any gradient button in the Attributes panel to open the Gradient Picker:

The Gradient Picker includes the following controls:

  • Gradient Type: Toggle between Linear and Radial gradients.
  • Color Stops: The preview bar displays all colors in the current gradient. You can add, edit, or remove color stops here.
  • Reverse: Click the reverse button to flip the order of the colors.
  • Gradient Library: Displays built-in presets and your saved favorite gradients.

Modify a Gradient

You can customize the current gradient in several ways:

  • Change Type: Switch between Linear and Radial.
  • Add a Color: Click anywhere on the gradient preview bar to add a new color stop.
  • Change a Color: Click an existing color stop to open the Color Picker and select a new color.
  • Adjust Position: Drag a color stop left or right to change its position in the gradient.
  • Remove a Color: Select a color stop and press the Delete key.
  • Reverse: Click the reverse button to flip the gradient.
  • On-Canvas Controls: Adjust the angle of a linear gradient or the center of a radial gradient by dragging the control knobs directly on the canvas.

Save to Favorites

To save a custom gradient for future use, click the + button at the bottom of the Gradient Picker. It will be added to your favorites library.

Use a Preset Gradient

Click any gradient in the library at the bottom of the panel to apply it instantly.
